Russian Braille Guide (Literary/Grade 1)

This guide summarizes how to enter capital letters and Latin characters using the Russian Braille system.

1. Cyrillic Capital Letters
   - Single Capital Letter: Press Dot 4 and Dot 5 before the letter.
   - Caps Lock (Multiple Capitals): Press Dot 4 and Dot 5 twice before the sequence.

2. Latin (English/International) Characters
   - Switch to Latin Mode (Lowercase): Press Dot 6.
   - Switch to Latin Mode (Uppercase): Press Dot 4 and Dot 6.

3. Special Latin Characters (available in Latin Mode)
   - The letter "ä" (a-umlaut): Use Dots 3-4-5.
   - The letter "ö" (o-umlaut): Use Dots 2-4-6.
   - The letter "ü" (u-umlaut): Use Dots 1-2-5-6.

Note: In standard Russian literary Braille, the system defaults to the Cyrillic alphabet. Using the prefixes above allows the user to switch modes or indicate capitalization for single letters or words.
